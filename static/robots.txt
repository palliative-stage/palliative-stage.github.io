# Scottish Palliative Care Guidelines, adapted for Israel.
# A collaboration between the Palliative NET group and the Tom Center
# (Hebrew University of Jerusalem).
#
# This site exists to put palliative care guidance in front of clinicians,
# patients and families in Israel. Crawling, indexing and citation are all
# welcome, including by AI assistants. Nothing here is restricted.
#
# Note: /search is intentionally NOT disallowed. It carries a noindex meta
# tag, and a crawler has to be able to fetch the page in order to see that
# tag. Disallowing it here would hide the noindex instead of enforcing it.

Allow: /
