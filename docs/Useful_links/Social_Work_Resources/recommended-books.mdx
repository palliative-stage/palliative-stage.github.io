# ספרים ומאמרים מומלצים

הרשימה מחולקת לעברית ולאנגלית, ובכל שפה מאורגנת לפי נושאים. ספר שתורגם לעברית מופיע בעברית, ושם המקור מצוין תחתיו. חלק מהפריטים הם מאמרים או מסות מכתבי עת, והם מסומנים ככאלה.

## בעברית

### ספרי יסוד מקצועיים בפליאציה ועבודה סוציאלית

- [בין החלמה לריפוי: מסע בטיפול הפליאטיבי](https://www.am-oved.co.il/%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9F_%D7%94%D7%97%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%99), ד"ר רוני צבר, עם עובד, 2022.
  - ספר מקור עברי על טיפול פליאטיבי, החלטות טיפול, שיח עם משפחות והמעבר מחשיבה של החלמה לחשיבה של ריפוי ואיכות חיים.
- להיות בן תמותה: הרפואה ומה שחשוב בסוף החיים
  - במקור: [Being Mortal: Medicine and What Matters in the End](https://atulgawande.com/book/being-mortal/), Atul Gawande, Metropolitan Books, 2014.
  - ספר יסוד נגיש על גבולות הרפואה, אוטונומיה, מוסדות טיפוליים והעדפות סוף חיים.

### שיחות סוף חיים, תקשורת וטיפול ממוקד-אדם

- לחיות עם המוות, אליזבת קובלר-רוס.
  - במקור: [On Death and Dying: What the Dying Have to Teach Doctors, Nurses, Clergy and Their Own Families](https://search.worldcat.org/title/325223194), Elisabeth Kubler-Ross, Macmillan, 1969.
  - ספר קלאסי על שיח עם חולים נוטים למות; חשוב לקרוא אותו גם מתוך מודעות לביקורת העכשווית על שימוש נוקשה במודל השלבים.
- חיים בצל המוות, אליזבת קובלר-רוס.
  - במקור: Living with Death and Dying, Elisabeth Kubler-Ross, Macmillan, 1981.
  - מתאים לעיסוק בבדידות המטופל והמשפחה, פחדים, תקווה והימנעות משיחה על מוות.
- המוות חשוב לחיים, אליזבת קובלר-רוס.
  - במקור: Death Is of Vital Importance: On Life, Death and Life After Death, Elisabeth Kubler-Ross, Station Hill Press, 1995.
  - אסופת הרצאות ומחשבות של קובלר-רוס על משמעות המוות בחיים.
- מי מת
  - במקור: [Who Dies?: An Investigation of Conscious Living and Conscious Dying](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2629422W/Who_dies), Stephen Levine and Ondrea Levine, Anchor Press, 1982.
  - ספר רוחני-חווייתי על נוכחות, חמלה והתמודדות עם פחד מוות.
- חוכמה משולחן המטבח: סיפורים שמרפאים
  - במקור: [Kitchen Table Wisdom: Stories That Heal](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3240345W/Kitchen_Table_Wisdom), Rachel Naomi Remen, Riverhead Books, 1996.
  - סיפורים קצרים מעבודתה של רופאה עם חולים, המדגישים הקשבה, משמעות וריפוי שאינו בהכרח החלמה.
- ברכות סבי
  - במקור: [My Grandfather's Blessings: Stories of Strength, Refuge, and Belonging](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/348373/my-grandfathers-blessings-by-rachel-naomi-remen/), Rachel Naomi Remen, Riverhead Books, 2000.
  - מתאים לחשיבה על נוכחות, חסד וקשר אנושי בעבודת טיפול.

### אבל, שכול, אובדן וטנטולוגיה

- טנטולוגיה: מדעי האובדן, השכול והאבל: נושאים נבחרים, הניה שנון קליין, שולמית קרייטלר ומיכל קרייטלר.
  - ספר מקור עברי למושגים מרכזיים בחקר מוות, אבל ושכול.
- נפש, אבל ושכול, אליעזר ויצטום.
  - עוסק בהיבטים נפשיים של שכול ואובדן ובהתמודדות טיפולית עם אבל.
- הפנים הרבות של האובדן והשכול, שמשון רובין, רות מלקינסון ואליעזר ויצטום.
  - מקור עברי מרכזי סביב אבל, קשר מתמשך עם הנפטר והמודל הדו-מסלולי.
  - באנגלית: Working with the Bereaved: Multiple Lenses on Loss and Mourning, Simon Shimshon Rubin, Ruth Malkinson, and Eliezer Witztum, Routledge, 2012.
- [אובדנים שאינם מוות](https://resling.co.il/product/%D7%90%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%95%D7%AA/), פרופ' נחמי באום, רסלינג, 2021.
  - מרחיב את שפת האובדן למצבים שאינם מוות, אך נושאים חוויית אבל משמעותית.
- הערבה הבוכיה
  - במקור: [The Weeping Willow: Encounters with Grief](https://lynnehalamish.com/books-by-lynne/), Lynne Dale Halamish and Doron Hermoni, Oxford University Press, 2007.
  - סיפורי מקרה וכלים מעשיים לשיחה עם ילדים, משפחות ואבלים.

## English

### Core professional books in palliative care and social work

- [The Oxford Textbook of Palliative Social Work](https://search.worldcat.org/title/710993070), edited by Terry Altilio and Shirley Otis-Green, Oxford University Press, 2011.
  - A central textbook of palliative social work, focused on biopsychosocial-spiritual assessment and intervention with patients and families.
- [Being Mortal: Medicine and What Matters in the End](https://atulgawande.com/book/being-mortal/), Atul Gawande, Metropolitan Books, 2014.
  - An accessible foundational book on the limits of medicine, autonomy, care institutions, and end-of-life preferences.
- [The Best Care Possible: A Physician's Quest to Transform Care Through the End of Life](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/308207/the-best-care-possible-by-ira-byock/), Ira Byock, Avery, 2012.
  - Presents the philosophy and practice of palliative care centered on the person and the family.
- [With the End in Mind: Dying, Death and Wisdom in an Age of Denial](https://www.williamcollinsbooks.co.uk/products/with-the-end-in-mind-dying-death-and-wisdom-in-an-age-of-denial-kathryn-mannix-9780008210892/), Kathryn Mannix, William Collins, 2017.
  - Clear case accounts by a palliative physician, especially useful for preparing families for the process of dying.
- Extreme Measures: Finding a Better Path to the End of Life, Jessica Nutik Zitter, Avery, 2017.
  - An intensive-care and palliative physician describes the move from automatic aggressive treatment to care that starts from the patient's values.
- Article: Run for your life: the reaction of some professionals to a person with cancer. Amir M, Kalemkerian GP. J Clin Oncol. 2003;21(19):3696-3699. doi: 10.1200/JCO.2003.01.107. PMID: 14512402.
  - A short article on how professionals react to meeting someone with a serious illness, relevant to staff teaching and reflection.

### End-of-life conversations, communication, and person-centered care

- [On Death and Dying: What the Dying Have to Teach Doctors, Nurses, Clergy and Their Own Families](https://search.worldcat.org/title/325223194), Elisabeth Kubler-Ross, Macmillan, 1969.
  - Hebrew translations and closely related titles have been published.
  - A classic on talking with dying patients; it should also be read with awareness of contemporary criticism of rigid use of the stages model.
- Living with Death and Dying, Elisabeth Kubler-Ross, Macmillan, 1981.
  - Suited to work on the patient's and family's loneliness, fears, hope, and avoidance of talking about death.
- Death Is of Vital Importance: On Life, Death and Life After Death, Elisabeth Kubler-Ross, Station Hill Press, 1995.
  - A collection of Kubler-Ross's lectures and thoughts on the meaning of death in life.
- [Who Dies?: An Investigation of Conscious Living and Conscious Dying](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2629422W/Who_dies), Stephen Levine and Ondrea Levine, Anchor Press, 1982.
  - A spiritual, experiential book on presence, compassion, and living with the fear of death.
- [Awake at the Bedside: Contemplative Teachings on Palliative and End-of-Life Care](https://awakeatthebedside.com/about-the-book/), edited by Koshin Paley Ellison and Matt Weingast, Wisdom Publications, 2016.
  - An anthology of professional and spiritual writing on a helpful presence at the bedside.
- [Kitchen Table Wisdom: Stories That Heal](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL3240345W/Kitchen_Table_Wisdom), Rachel Naomi Remen, Riverhead Books, 1996.
  - Short stories from a physician's work with patients, emphasizing listening, meaning, and healing that is not necessarily a cure.
- [My Grandfather's Blessings: Stories of Strength, Refuge, and Belonging](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/348373/my-grandfathers-blessings-by-rachel-naomi-remen/), Rachel Naomi Remen, Riverhead Books, 2000.
  - Useful for thinking about presence, kindness, and human connection in care work.
- Article: How to Respond to People in Crisis: Comfort In; Dump Out, Deborah L. Davis, Psychology Today, 2018.
  - The ring theory of support is useful for guiding teams and families: comfort goes in toward the person at the center of the crisis, and venting goes out.

### Grief, bereavement, loss, and thanatology

- Working with the Bereaved: Multiple Lenses on Loss and Mourning, Simon Shimshon Rubin, Ruth Malkinson, and Eliezer Witztum, Routledge, 2012.
  - A professional English version of the authors' work on grief, bereavement, and a continuing bond with the person who died.
- [The Weeping Willow: Encounters with Grief](https://lynnehalamish.com/books-by-lynne/), Lynne Dale Halamish and Doron Hermoni, Oxford University Press, 2007.
  - Case stories and practical tools for talking with children, families, and bereaved people.
- Article: Awareness of Dying Remains Relevant after Fifty Years, Tom Andrews and Alvita K. Nathaniel, Grounded Theory Review, December 2015.
  - An updated review of Glaser and Strauss's classic research on awareness of approaching death.
- Meaning Reconstruction and the Experience of Loss, edited by Robert A. Neimeyer, American Psychological Association, 2001.
  - An important professional source for understanding grief through meaning-making, and not only through "stages".
- Article: Get Out of My Potato Patch: A Biased View of Death and Dying, Mona Wasow, Health & Social Work, 1984.
  - An article on death and dying, written from a personal and professional point of view.

### Meaning, spirituality, and existential coping

- [Staring at the Sun: Overcoming the Terror of Death](https://www.yalom.com/staring-at-the-sun/), Irvin D. Yalom, Jossey-Bass, 2008.
  - An existential therapy book on the fear of death, regret, meaning, and living with an awareness of finitude.
- Creatures of a Day: And Other Tales of Psychotherapy, Irvin D. Yalom, Basic Books, 2015.
  - Short therapy stories about finitude, relationship, loneliness, and meaning.
  - Includes the story Get Your Own Damn Fatal Illness: Homage to Ellie.
- Man's Search for Meaning, Viktor E. Frankl, 1946.
  - A basic source for logotherapy and for meaning in the face of suffering.
- [The Tibetan Book of Living and Dying](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/the-tibetan-book-of-living-and-dying-sogyal-rinpoche), Sogyal Rinpoche, Rider / HarperSanFrancisco, 1992.
  - A Buddhist spiritual source on compassion, awareness, and accompanying a dying person.
- [Fearless Death: Buddhist Wisdom on the Art of Dying](https://www.lama-ole-nydahl.org/books.html), Lama Ole Nydahl, Diamond Way Press, 2013.
  - A Buddhist spiritual reading on death, fear, awareness, and parting.
- [Dying to Be Me: My Journey from Cancer, to Near Death, to True Healing](https://www.hayhouse.com/dying-to-be-me-1), Anita Moorjani, Hay House, 2012.
  - A spiritual memoir of illness, a near-death experience, and healing; suitable for critical reading alongside clinical sources.
- Many Lives, Many Masters, Brian L. Weiss, 1988.
  - A popular spiritual book on reincarnation and regression therapy. It is not a clinical palliative source, but it may help in understanding the beliefs of patients and families.
- Does the Soul Survive?: A Jewish Journey to Belief in Afterlife, Past Lives and Living with Purpose, Rabbi Elie Kaplan Spitz, 2001.
  - A Jewish spiritual source for conversations about faith, continuity, and meaning.
- Encyclopaedia of Jewish Medical Ethics, Dr Avraham Steinberg.
  - A broad source on ethical and halakhic questions in medicine, including what to tell the patient and the care of the dying patient.

### Memoirs, testimony, and literature

- [When Breath Becomes Air](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/258507/when-breath-becomes-air-by-paul-kalanithi/), Paul Kalanithi, Random House, 2016.
  - A memoir by a neurosurgeon with advanced lung cancer, on identity, professionalism, family, and death.
- [Komt een vrouw bij de dokter](https://metadata.isbn.nl/1623006/komt-een-vrouw-bij-de-dokter.html), Kluun, Podium, 2003.
  - English edition: Love Life, translated by Shaun Whiteside, St. Martin's Griffin, 2007.
  - A semi-autobiographical novel about a relationship, illness, infidelity, treatment, and the end of life.
- [An Interrupted Life: The Diaries, 1941-1943; and Letters from Westerbork](https://archive.org/details/interruptedlifed00hill), Etty Hillesum, Dutch original 1981; English translation 1983.
  - Diaries and letters on inner freedom, meaning, and the human spirit in extreme conditions.
- [The Death of Ivan Ilyich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Death_of_Ivan_Ilyich), Leo Tolstoy, 1886.
  - A short literary work on loneliness, denial, pain, and a late recognition at the end of life.
- [Tuesdays with Morrie](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/1588/tuesdays-with-morrie-by-mitch-albom/), Mitch Albom, Doubleday, 1997.
  - Conversations between a student and his dying teacher about life, relationships, dependence, and parting.
- A Very Easy Death, Simone de Beauvoir, 1964.
  - A frank book in which de Beauvoir accompanies her mother's death and looks at the meeting of body, family, and the medical system.
- The Book of Dahlia, Elisa Albert, 2008.
  - A dark novel about serious illness at a young age, resistance to "positive" narratives, and family relationships.
- The Last Days of Rabbit Hayes, Anna McPartlin.
  - A novel about eight days in the life of a young woman in hospice and the family around her.

  - A collection of poems in which Abba Kovner records his involvement in life during advanced illness.

  - A personal and medical reading on illness, healing, and meaning.
